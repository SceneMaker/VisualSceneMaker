A flow describes a sequence of actions in a particular configuration represented by nodes and their connections.
Actions are executed by agents and grouped into input, processing, and output categories.
Input agents acquire information from outside the SceneFlow and update variables in the background, for example ASR or facial-expression recognition.
Processing agents transform or interpret data to support decision-making, for example LLMs, affect simulation, user modeling, or logging.
Output agents control external presentation or actuation channels, for example socially interactive agents, text-to-speech, or 3D environments.
Active flows are started when "Play" is pressed.
Non-active flows remain idle.
